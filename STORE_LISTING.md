# Chrome Web Store submission notes

## Single purpose

Browser Time Travel helps users find and reopen pages from their own Chrome browsing history using a spoken memory.

## Store description

People remember moments, not URLs. Say what you remember—such as “the video I watched yesterday”—and Browser Time Travel searches your local Chrome history and opens the best match.

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, analytics, tracking, or history uploads.

## Privacy disclosures to complete in the Developer Dashboard

- **Browsing activity:** Collected and used only to search and reopen pages the user requests. It is not transmitted by this extension.
- **Audio:** Microphone input is used only after the user starts voice search. Chrome’s speech-recognition service produces the transcript; the extension does not store or upload audio.
- **Data sale / advertising:** No data is sold, transferred for advertising, or used for personalized advertising.

Use the published `privacy.html` URL in the Dashboard’s Privacy Policy field. Keep these dashboard disclosures aligned with the extension’s actual behavior and privacy policy.
